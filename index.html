About Ben

Ben Noble helps California cities and counties turn planning goals into clear, workable policies and regulations.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, he brings more than 25 years of experience to zoning codes, general plans, neighborhood plans, and public outreach. His work spans urban centers, small towns, and rural communities.

Ben works closely with staff and residents to understand local priorities, explain the choices, and write plans people can use. He pays particular attention to how the rules will work in practice, balancing community benefits with the costs of implementation.

Background

Ben has run an independent practice since 2014. He previously served as an Associate Principal at PlaceWorks, formerly Design, Community & Environment, where he oversaw the firm’s zoning practice.
