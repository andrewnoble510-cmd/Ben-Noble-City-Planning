Special planning studies

Some planning questions call for a focused study rather than a whole new plan.

Ben helps local agencies consider the tools available to carry out their goals. His experience includes transfer of development rights, complete-community planning, and targeted policy work.

Regulation is one option among several. His approach also considers direct government action, economic incentives, and information or education, with attention to public benefits and the costs of implementation.
