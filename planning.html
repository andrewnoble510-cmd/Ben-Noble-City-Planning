Services

Comprehensive planning

Ben helps communities decide how they want to grow and put those choices into a usable plan.

His comprehensive planning work includes general plans, housing elements, local coastal programs, and focused policy amendments. He works with local agencies to connect broad community goals with policies that can guide everyday decisions.

The work ranges from citywide plans to a specific policy question. Ben brings experience in urban centers, small towns, and rural communities, with an emphasis on clear writing and a practical path to implementation.

How Ben can help

  • General plans and housing elements
  • Local coastal program updates
  • Targeted general plan and policy amendments

Project experience

Coastal planning

Arcata Local Coastal Program

Ben’s work on Arcata’s Local Coastal Program focuses on a clearer, more focused set of coastal policies and regulations. The assignment brings the Coastal Land Use Element and Coastal Zoning Code into closer alignment with Coastal Act requirements and the City’s vision.

The approach emphasizes the provisions needed to protect coastal resources and support a straightforward review process.
