Services

Specific & area plans

Ben helps communities plan for change at the neighborhood and district scale.

Area plans bring land use, building design, and local priorities into focus for a particular place. Ben’s work pairs planning and urban design with the regulations needed to carry a plan forward.

His assignments include mixed-use districts, transit-oriented areas, and objective design standards. Public discussion helps identify the choices; clear policies and standards explain how future development should fit.

How Ben can help

  • Specific plans, area plans, and district plans
  • Objective design standards
  • Zoning and implementation programs for planned areas

Project experience

Mixed-use planning

Benicia Eastern Gateway

Ben led the consultant team for a new mixed-use district near Military East and East Fifth Street. The project pairs by-right housing with objective zoning and design standards, creating a clearer route for development on vacant and underused land.

Community outreach explored housing choices, infill opportunities, and how new buildings could fit alongside existing homes.
