Regional planning & coordination

Planning often extends beyond a city boundary. Ben helps local agencies work across those boundaries.

His experience includes countywide plans, regional sustainability work, and coordination among local governments. These assignments connect local land use priorities with broader transportation, housing, and growth decisions.

Ben brings the same practical approach to regional work that he brings to local plans: understand the issues, make the choices clear, and help agencies move the work forward together.
